01

Color & Light

Dark-mode first. The brand's deep taupe settles into a warm espresso-taupe ground (nudged toward the personal-brand Deep Taupe family, never black, never green) so the signature electric yellow reads as light emitting from a screen. Bone and linen carry warmth into text; gold and champagne add metallic restraint. Spark colors (cinder, ash) appear only in data-viz line art.

03

Motion Language

One signature easing curve governs everything: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) — a fast, confident departure that settles softly, like a film cut resolving. Motion is mobile-first: every effect below is touch/scroll-driven, not hover-dependent, so it translates to phones. All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Scroll Reveal

Content rises 28px and fades in, staggered, as it enters the viewport. The core entrance for every section. ScrollTrigger, batched for performance on mobile.

Magnetic Pull

Primary CTAs drift toward the cursor and snap back (desktop), and pulse-glow on tap (mobile). Communicates "this is the action."

Liquid Sheen

A slow specular streak crosses every glass surface — the "liquid glass" tell. Pauses under reduced-motion. GPU-only transform.

Odometer Roll

Stat digits roll into place like a mechanical counter on scroll-in — numbers feel earned, not stated. From the cinematic kit.

Spotlight Border

A shared grid where the border glows under the pointer (desktop) and the active card brightens on scroll (mobile). Unifies cards into one surface.

Kinetic Marquee

A credential strip that scrolls continuously and accelerates with scroll velocity. Adds energy without imagery.

04

Liquid Glass

The defining surface treatment. Layered translucency (4.5–7% bone fill), a 1px luminous stroke, an inner top highlight, and a roaming specular streak. It gives depth and a premium "frosted screen" quality while keeping text legible on the dark ground. Three tiers:

Tier 1 · Base

Quiet glass

For large panels and the registration modal. Low fill, soft stroke. Recedes behind content.

Tier 2 · Bright

Active glass

For hovered/selected states and the featured pricing tier. Gold-tinted stroke, higher fill.

Tier 3 · Emissive

Lit glass

Reserved for the single primary CTA moment. Gold gradient bleed behind the glass for an emitting-light feel.
